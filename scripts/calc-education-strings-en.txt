Unit Economics
Unlike CPM (Cost Per Mille) which charges per impression, CPC ensures you only pay when users take action by clicking. This makes CPC ideal for performance-based campaigns focused on driving traffic, leads, or conversions.
Unlike LTV:CAC ratio which shows total value, payback period focuses on time to breakeven. A shorter payback means faster reinvestment in growth. According to Bessemer Venture Partners, the benchmark is 12 months or less for healthy SaaS companies.
Unlike NRR, GRR cannot exceed 100% and reveals the true impact of churn without expansion revenue masking problems. A healthy SaaS business should retain 85-95% of recurring revenue year-over-year, with best-in-class achieving 95-100%.
Unlike NRR, expansion can't hide retention problems in GRR
Unlike channel-specific ROAS, MER gives you a holistic, big-picture view of your entire marketing performance across all channels combined.
Unlike eCommerce LTV, SaaS LTV uses monthly churn rate to estimate customer lifespan. According to Churnkey and industry benchmarks, a 3:1 LTV:CAC ratio is the gold standard for sustainable SaaS growth.
Unlike paid ads that stop when you stop paying, SEO provides compounding returns over time with ongoing optimization.
Unlike revenue-focused metrics, this simplified eCommerce LTV formula factors in gross margin to show actual profit contribution. According to Shopify, a good LTV:CAC ratio is 3:1, meaning each customer should generate 3x what you spent to acquire them.
Unlike traditional CAC which blends costs of acquiring both new and returning customers, nCAC isolates the true cost of expanding your customer base. This distinction is critical for understanding real growth versus revenue from existing customers.
